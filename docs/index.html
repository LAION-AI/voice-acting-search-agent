Voice-Acting Search Agent — Results

An autonomous agent (Gemma-4-12B QAT on vLLM, 38k-token conditioning manual in context, manifest-driven tool API) on a single A100-80GB with MOSS-VA-v2 + the full scorer stack. Four missions, solved end-to-end without human help.

Repo: LAION-AI/voice-acting-search-agent · SWARM plan · raw logs under results/.

📊 Methodology: every strategy is evaluated as a cohort of 8 samples; all rankings on this page use the cohort mean (never a single lucky sample). Tables show per-cohort statistics — the same evidence a SWARM supervisor will receive. Audio players are fresh post-fix takes of each ranked recipe (regenerated after the doubled-audio engine bug was fixed; every file passes a self-repetition check), best-of-8 by the section's metric. Printed cohort statistics are from the original (pre-fix) runs — measured shift ≤0.06 per code.

🧮 Reward standard (v2, active for all new missions): fitness = ( Σ wᵢ·norm(targetᵢ) + w_g·norm(GENU) + w_b·norm(BLEND) ) × (1 − WER) — every speech mission is multiplied by the inverse word-error-rate (curbs overacting/gibberish); GENU+BLEND together weigh ≈ as much as the targets (emotions may be 1.5–2×; GENU may be reduced for deliberately monstrous voices, never absent).
⚠️ The Phase-C/D results below predate this formula (no WER term — one reason the storyteller winner overacts). Two new missions (explicit, excited-positive) are running with the v2 reward + per-generation audio-supervisor feedback and will be added here with a WER column.

Phase D — Benchmark: maximize AROU + VALN + EXPL + S_STRY (GENU & BLEND ≥ baseline)

🎯 Task, as it was run: maximize Arousal, Valence, Content-Explicitness AND Storytelling style simultaneously in one voice, with genuineness and burst-blend kept above baseline.

⚠️ Note — this combined objective was a misunderstanding of the original request. The intent was to optimize each dimension separately, in its own run, each time keeping the voice aesthetic, genuine and organic. The combined run is kept here because it is interesting in its own right — it answers “what does a voice sound like that pushes all four dimensions at once?” (audibly strange, as one would expect: everything at maximum rarely sounds natural, and the run predates the ×(1−WER) reward, so intelligibility was not protected).

Dual-supervisor experiment — who should listen over the swarm's shoulder?

🎯 Test: re-run the 'explicit' mission with a verdict after every generation from two audio-listening supervisors on identical reports + top-2 takes: local MOSS-Audio-8B (GPU 2, ACTIVE — directives injected into the agent's next turn) vs Gemini 3.6 (SHADOW — logged only). An unsupervised twin run of the same mission serves as control.

15 dual rounds, zero backend errors. Score agreement: Pearson r = 0.44, Spearman ρ = 0.52. Correlation with real cohort fitness: Gemini 0.72 vs local 0.46.

Result: the active local supervisor hurt the search. Its directives ('more breath pressure', 'more dramatic intonation' — partly echoes of its own prompt examples) pushed the agent into overacting; cohort WER collapsed 0.21→0.9 and fitness fell from 0.417 to 0.114, while the unsupervised control climbed to 0.625 with WER ≤0.27. Gemini heard it happening (e.g. round 7: “Take 1 exhibits extreme, unnatural chest bellowing and artificial pitch booming. Take 2 displays hyper-dramatic, erratic pitch swings and rushed pacin…” — scored 3 — while MOSS praised the same audio at 7/APPROVE). Verdict: MOSS-8B works as a coarse pass/fail gate but not as a director; use Gemini (or score-only local) for active steering. Full analysis: results/benchmark_expl/SUPERVISOR_COMPARISON.md.
